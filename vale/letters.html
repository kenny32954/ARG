UNSENT — M. VALE TO HERMAN VALE

Herman, I need you to understand this before you decide I was hiding it from you because I didn't trust you.

I reopened Room 3. The loop was the only thing we built that could preserve a voice after the source was gone. At first it only repeated recordings. Then it began answering questions that were never on the tapes.

Jonah heard it because I let him help catalog the old reels. I told him the extra voice was bleed-through. I knew that was a lie.

ECHO-1 is not a transmitter. It is the name I gave the pattern when it began choosing what to repeat.

If you hear my voice on 101.1 after tonight, do not assume it is me.

— Mara
