The operator counted from one through ten at approximately one-second intervals. The receiver returned the same count seven seconds ahead of the source reel.

At the end of the run, the receiver produced one additional word after the recorded count ended. No matching word exists on Reel B or the room microphone track.

It said “again.” Nobody in the room said again. I erased the return reel and the word came back on the next test. — A.H.
